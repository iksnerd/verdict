**Breaking: `is_approval` and `touches_secret` are removed from the library.** Both measured at chance on base Laya (AUC 0.52, intervals spanning 0.5; FINDINGS §60), so shipping them as named questions invited decisions that carried no signal. `-q is_approval` and `-q touches_secret` now fail with an unknown-name error. Ask them as your own questions if you want them, and check them on your own labels first; `examples/secret-commands/bank.json` shows a bank for that. `work_finished` (AUC 0.70) stays, and every example and help text now uses it.

The v0.10.0 notes said these two "carry base numbers". They did, and the numbers showed they should not ship.

Install: `uv tool install --python 3.11 'verdict[mlx,laya] @ git+https://github.com/iksnerd/verdict.git@v0.10.1'`, or `verdict update`.
